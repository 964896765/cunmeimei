The phone is the sole focal object. Match a premium friendly soft-clay 3D render with the cozy pastel blush/pearl/lavender materials and soft warm studio lighting of the user's housing category illustrations. The model should have convincing solid volume, rounded physical bevels and subtle material highlights. Place it centered, fully visible, large enough for a tiny two-column category card, taking approximately 78 percent of the canvas in height. Include a small diffuse natural gray-mauve contact shadow beneath it, fading completely into transparency. Transparent alpha background, isolated object, clean edges. No square backdrop, no panel, no pedestal, no room environment, no labels, letters, numbers, brand logos, recycle badge, icons or watermark. It will be composited directly onto a pale pink card, so the whole outer canvas must remain genuinely transparent. Square composition.
```
